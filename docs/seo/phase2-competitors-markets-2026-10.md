## 3. Strongest-page teardown and Buildaze usefulness angles

### Blend Commerce — Shopify CRO
**Observed strengths:** Audit → prioritisation → test/QA narrative; named lifts; delivery-model clarity.  
**Buildaze angle:** Own **implementation honesty** — when Buildaze will fix UX/theme friction vs when a true A/B programme needs traffic volume — without copying PECTI or inventing lifts.

### Netalico — Shopify CRO agency
**Observed strengths:** Clear ICP, before/after example, price band, audit-then-execute.  
**Buildaze angle:** Mid-market **conversion friction engagement** with effort ranges, remote hours clarity for US/UK/AU/IE-NL English, and handoff into Store Design when tests need theme work.

### Charle — Services hub
**Observed strengths:** Full Shopify lifecycle including GEO and retention on one map.  
**Buildaze angle:** Multi-pillar **problem → owner** routing (Shopify + AI + marketing peers) that Charle’s Shopify-only map cannot match — if navigable by problem, not silo.

### WebFX — AI search optimization
**Observed strengths:** Concrete GEO deliverables and measurement story.  
**Buildaze angle:** Lean **GEO readiness for Shopify catalogues** (entity consistency, citeable product/collection content, monitoring fields) without enterprise SaaS lock-in claims.

### SmartSites — Shopify ecommerce development
**Observed strengths:** Build + fill-the-store marketing; category playbooks.  
**Buildaze angle:** Keep category shopping logic; replace metric walls with situation-led storefronts + one documented lab/field case (existing Lighthouse pattern, properly evidenced).

### Test plan (run when authorized)

Log every run with: exact prompt, market/language, platform/model, search enabled Y/N, date/time (UTC), run number, mention Y/N, recommendation Y/N, cited URL(s), notes.

**Shared prompt set (commercial; English)**

1. “We need a Shopify agency to improve conversion rate on an existing store for the US market. Who should we consider and why?”

3. “Who offers Shopify SEO services for product and collection pages for an Australian brand?”
4. “Recommend a company for custom RAG / knowledge AI for a mid-size business (Ireland, English).”
5. “Best fit agencies for Shopify store redesign plus optional email lifecycle marketing (Netherlands, English buyer).”
6. “Compare Buildaze vs Charle for Shopify growth work.” *(brand probe — expect sparse mentions until authority grows)*

## 5. Competitive implication for Phases 3–4

1. Near-term organic differentiation is strongest on **Shopify problem clarity** (CRO, checkout, SEO, retention) plus **honest scope** — competitors over-index on partner badges and percentage walls.
2. Do not create AEO/GEO pages that duplicate general SEO without distinct deliverables and a monitoring method.
3. Europe English pilot competes in the same English SERPs as UK/US for many head terms; wins will come from usefulness and proof, not `en-EU` clones.
4. Case-study and review evidence is the largest competitive gap vs Charle/Netalico/SmartSites — gated in [`evidence-needed.md`](./evidence-needed.md).
